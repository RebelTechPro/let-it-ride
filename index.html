How to play & strategy

You place three equal bets and get three cards. Two community cards are dealt face down. Your best five-card hand is your three cards plus both community cards. There's no dealer hand to beat — you're paid on the paytable.

  • Decision 1: after seeing your 3 cards, pull back bet 1 or let it ride. The first community card is then turned.
  • Decision 2: with 4 cards known, pull back bet 2 or let it ride. The second community card is then turned.
  • The $ bet always stays. Any bets still up are paid at the paytable odds, or lost if the hand is below a pair of 10s.
  • The optional 3-card bonus pays on your first three cards only.

Ride on 3 cards with: a pair of 10s or better, three of a kind, three to a royal flush, three suited in a row (except 2-3-4 and A-2-3), three to a straight flush with one gap and a 10 or higher, or three to a straight flush with two gaps and two cards 10 or higher.

Ride on 4 cards with: any paying hand, four to a flush, an open-ended straight draw, or four cards 10 or higher to a straight.

Otherwise, pull it back. In the hand log, a decision that went against this strategy is underlined in orange.
